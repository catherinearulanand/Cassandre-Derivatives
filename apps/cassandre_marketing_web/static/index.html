Controls, not autonomy

Four structural guarantees, enforced—not promised.

01

Maker-checker everywhere

No submitter approves their own material change. Every rule version, waiver, and release requires an independent second party.

02

Deterministic calculation

Margin, eligibility, and allocation math run on versioned deterministic code. A model may propose; it never computes the authoritative figure.

03

Source-linked evidence

Every material field carries its source document, page span, evidence period, and reviewer status back to the original record.

04

Signed, replayable audit

Content-addressed releases and signed envelopes mean every past decision can be reproduced from its exact input versions.

Evidence, not inference

Missing evidence is shown as missing—never assumed compliant.

A stale statement, an unresolved margin, a debtor past its approved ageing cut-off: each becomes a distinct, visible state—compliant, near-threshold, indeterminate, or breach—rather than a single collapsed "risk score."
